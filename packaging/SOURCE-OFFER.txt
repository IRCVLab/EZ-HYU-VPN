HYU VPN internal lab written source offer

For at least three years from internal distribution, the lab maintainer who provided this DMG will provide the corresponding source for copied GPL/LGPL runtime components on request, at no more than the reasonable cost of physically transferring the source.

Primary upstream source locations:
- OpenConnect / libopenconnect: https://www.openconnect-vpn.net/
- p11-kit: https://p11-glue.github.io/p11-glue/p11-kit.html
- stoken: https://github.com/cernekee/stoken
- gnuTLS: https://www.gnutls.org/
- nettle / hogweed: https://www.lysator.liu.se/~nisse/nettle/
- GNU MP: https://gmplib.org/
- GNU gettext: https://www.gnu.org/software/gettext/
- GNU libidn2: https://www.gnu.org/software/libidn/
- GNU libunistring: https://www.gnu.org/software/libunistring/

- oath-toolkit / liboath / oathtool: https://www.nongnu.org/oath-toolkit/
